How to read these ratings

The solver is the fixed anchor at 1000 Elo. Ratings use all recorded arena results, not just the match shown here. Every player has one 5×5 bomb per game. Regularization stabilizes estimates when evidence is sparse; the anchor defines the scale, not a guaranteed level of performance.

The ± value is the estimated 95% confidence half-width. Wider intervals mean less certainty, especially with few games. Overlapping intervals are not strong evidence of a difference. The fixed anchor has no estimated rating uncertainty.
